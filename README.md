## What is this?

Instead of reading through a resume, you can have a conversation. This is a personal chatbot that answers questions about Gabriel, an Information Technology student at NJIT. It's built for recruiters, classmates and anyone curious about him.

Type a question the way you'd ask a person, and get a short, friendly answer.

## What you can ask

- 🎓 **Education**: *"What does Gabriel study?"*, *"When does he graduate?"*
- 💼 **Experience**: *"What's his work experience?"*
- 💻 **Skills**: *"Which programming languages does he know?"*, *"What's his tech stack?"*
- 🌍 **Languages**: *"What languages does he speak?"*
- ⚽ **Life outside work**: *"What sports does he play?"*, *"Tell me a fun fact about him"*

## Good to know

- **It sticks to the facts.** Answers come only from Gabriel's own information. If it doesn't know something, it says so and points you to his LinkedIn.
- **Ask in your language.** It replies in English, Portuguese, Spanish and more.
- **Works anywhere.** Use it on your phone or computer, in light or dark mode.

## Built with

Next.js and TypeScript, [Claude](https://www.anthropic.com/claude) by Anthropic for the answers, and hosted on Vercel.

<details>
<summary>Run it locally</summary>

```bash

```

Then open http://localhost:5173. To change what the bot knows, edit [`api/_profile.ts`](api/_profile.ts).
